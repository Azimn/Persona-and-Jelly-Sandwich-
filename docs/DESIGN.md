# Design Notes

## The subject, not the loop, is primary

The loop exists because every perception, pressure, action, and consequence belongs to one continuing individual. A component may be replaced; ownership may not.

## Homeostasis without fake biology

The runtime uses a small collection of bounded values rather than attempting physiological realism. These values are experiential controls. Their purpose is to create continuous imbalance and regulation:

- low energy and high fatigue bias rest
- hunger and thirst create unresolved bodily pressure
- low comfort and warmth bias self-regulation
- curiosity and restlessness bias exploration
- loneliness biases orientation toward contact
- safety, focus, and satisfaction shape readiness

## One decision path

Habits, needs, emotions, memories, and relationships are influences on one action selection step. They are not independent agents and do not generate competing hidden conversations.

## Objective and subjective records

An `Event` is supplied by the host. The organism converts it into an `OwnedEvent`: what the event meant to this particular subject. A later `Consequence` is supplied by the host after action resolution. This prevents the character from declaring its own actions successful or fabricating world changes.

## Self-narrative is evidence-bound

Repeated memory tags can form a `NarrativeClaim`. Every claim retains memory IDs as evidence. Claims are cautious generalizations, not immutable identity facts. A later contrary pattern can alter them.

## Character ownership

The generic renderer has only emergency neutral fallbacks. Complete characters keep authored language in their cartridge. The same boundary applies to needs, sensitivities, preferences, habits, and idle activities.
