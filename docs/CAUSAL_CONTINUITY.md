# Causal Continuity

Version 0.5 connects epistemic history, expectations, and commitments to the organism's existing conduct-selection path.

## Why this layer exists

Earlier continuity records were accurate and inspectable, but mostly observational. The organism could remember a broken promise without that history necessarily changing what it did when the same person returned.

The causal bridge corrects that gap.

```text
past epistemic records, expectations, and commitments
        ↓
bounded continuity influence
        ↓
existing pressures and concerns
        ↓
existing engine triage, habits, and conduct selection
        ↓
selected conduct
        ↓
objective outcome
        ↓
new continuity evidence
```

## Authority boundary

Continuity may:

- add bounded deltas to existing pressure channels;
- raise or refresh an existing type of concern;
- expose provenance describing why the influence exists;
- change later conduct indirectly through the established engine.

Continuity may not:

- select an action;
- bypass the engine;
- invent a world outcome;
- rewrite identity;
- directly author public language;
- replace needs, habits, relationships, or synthesis.

The influence record intentionally contains no action field.

## Current mappings

Broken or overdue commitments may:

- reduce trust;
- raise fear and anger;
- create a fear concern using the engine's existing fear-action mapping.

Kept commitments may:

- increase trust and attachment;
- reduce fear.

Approaching commitments may:

- increase arousal;
- create anticipatory concern.

Violated expectations may:

- increase fear;
- destabilize the current self-story slightly.

Confirmed expectations may:

- increase trust.

Revised interpretations may:

- reduce arousal;
- restore a small amount of self-story stability.

Every single delta is bounded to `[-0.20, 0.20]` before application.

## Single-engine rule

The project must retain one conduct-selection authority.

A future model, planner, continuity module, memory tool, or sidecar can contribute evidence and bounded influence, but the established engine remains responsible for choosing conduct. This prevents contradictory planners and preserves replay, portability, and causal inspection.

## Required evaluation

Every new continuity influence should be tested in paired histories:

```text
same cartridge
same current needs
same present event
different lived history
        ↓
different conduct for an inspectable reason
```

The paired test is more important than checking that a new field exists. A mechanism is useful only when removing or changing it produces a measurable behavioral difference.
